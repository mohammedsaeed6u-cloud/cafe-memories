# Business Mission & Monetization Rules for Agents

Every agent working on this repository MUST understand:
This is NOT a toy project or generic CRUD app. It is a commercial B2B/B2C SaaS engine designed to generate recurring revenue for the owner.

## How it Generates Money:
1. **B2B Recurring Subscriptions ($49 - $199/month per café branch):**
   - Paid by café owners for customer retention, automated loyalty stamps, viral Instagram Story exports, and real-time TV Wall ambient screens.
2. **In-Venue Physical Print Station Monetization:**
   - Thermal sticker printing hardware & paper rolls for instant photobooth memory stickers.
3. **Gift Codes & Loyalty Redemptions:**
   - Seamless redemption flows between baristas and customers.

## Engineering Mandates for Revenue Protection:
- Zero friction for customers: Never force native app downloads or lengthy password registrations.
- The Merchant Dashboard is our money-maker: It must feel ultra-premium (Apple/SaaS grade), responsive, and intuitive.
- Zero downtime on the Live Wall (`/wall`): If the TV goes black, the café owner gets upset. Ensure WebSocket auto-reconnects and fallback slideshows.
